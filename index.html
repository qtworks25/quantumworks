Medical Story Q

Medical Story Q is our educational YouTube channel. Each episode answers an everyday question about the human body in English, with Spanish and Korean subtitles and descriptions.

Channel: youtube.com/@medicalstoryq

Content on the channel is for general education only and is not medical advice.

Medical Story Q Publisher

Medical Story Q Publisher is our internal publishing tool. It uses YouTube API Services to manage our own channel only: after a video is uploaded, it sets the title and description with translations, adds caption tracks, sets the thumbnail, adds the video to our series playlist, and schedules the publish time.

The tool is used only by our own staff. It is not offered to the public and does not access any other channel.
