Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This policy explains how Quantum Works (주식회사 퀀텀웍스, "we") handles information in Medical Story Q Publisher (the "Tool") and on this website.

1. YouTube API Services

The Tool uses YouTube API Services. By using the Tool you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Who uses the Tool

The Tool is an internal tool. It is used only by our own staff to manage our own YouTube channel, Medical Story Q. It is not offered to the public, and it does not access any other person's channel or account.

3. Information the Tool accesses

When our channel owner signs in with Google (OAuth 2.0), the Tool can access the following data of our own channel through YouTube API Services:

  • Video details: title, description, translations (localizations), category, privacy status and scheduled publish time.
  • Caption tracks attached to our videos.
  • Our playlists and the videos in them.
  • Read-only YouTube Analytics reports for our own channel (permission granted for future internal reporting; not used at present).

The Tool does not access comments, subscribers' personal information, or any data of other channels.

4. How we use it

We use this access only to publish our own videos: setting titles, descriptions and translations, uploading caption tracks and thumbnails, adding videos to our playlist, and scheduling publication. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

5. What we store

  • Sign-in token. The OAuth token is stored only on our operator's computer, outside our source code, and is protected by the computer's user account.
  • Publishing records. For each of our videos we keep the video ID, privacy status and scheduled time in our internal production records.

We do not sell, rent or share this information with third parties. Data is sent only to Google through YouTube API Services.

6. Cookies

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. The Tool does not place cookies or other data on anyone else's device.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep the token and publishing records only while our channel authorization is active and the records are needed. If the authorization is revoked, or on request, we delete the stored token and data obtained through YouTube API Services within 7 days.

8. Revoking access

Access granted to the Tool can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

9. Children

The Tool and this website are not directed to children and do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.
